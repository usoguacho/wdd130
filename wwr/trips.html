Featured Expeditions

Colorado River — Grand Canyon

Class IV–V

📍 Grand Canyon, Arizona  |  7 Days  |  From $2,800/person

The ultimate bucket-list river run. Carve through one of the seven natural wonders of the world as towering canyon walls rise over a mile above you. Our expert guides navigate legendary rapids like Lava Falls and Crystal, while evenings are spent camping on sandy beaches beneath a canopy of stars. This is raw, unfiltered wilderness — and it will change you.

  • 225 miles of canyon river
  • Camp on pristine riverside beaches
  • All meals and gear included
  • Max 16 guests per expedition

Zambezi River — Victoria Falls

Class V

📍 Zimbabwe / Zambia  |  5 Days  |  From $3,200/person

Just below the thundering mist of Victoria Falls lies one of the most intense stretches of whitewater on earth. The Zambezi's Class V rapids — with names like "The Terminator" and "Gnashing Jaws of Death" — demand respect and reward courage. Safari game drives and cultural village visits round out this once-in-a-lifetime African adventure.

  • Departs directly below Victoria Falls
  • Optional safari game drive
  • Prior Class IV experience required
  • Max 12 guests per expedition

Salmon River — River of No Return

Class III–IV

📍 Central Idaho  |  6 Days  |  From $1,950/person

Flowing through the largest wilderness in the lower 48, the Salmon River delivers exhilarating rapids, world-class fishing, and stunning high-desert scenery. With a mix of Class III and IV whitewater, this trip is ideal for those ready to step up from beginner runs without going full expedition-mode. Hot springs, wildlife, and campfire cooking await.

  • 79-mile wilderness corridor
  • Natural hot springs access
  • Excellent for families & groups
  • Max 20 guests per expedition

Futaleufú River — Patagonia

Class IV–V

📍 Chilean Patagonia  |  8 Days  |  From $3,800/person

Widely regarded as one of the most beautiful rivers on earth, the "Fu" combines turquoise glacial water with relentless Class V rapids set against dramatic Andean peaks. Between runs, kayak instruction, horseback riding, and zip-lining fill the days. A true adventure traveler's grand slam — scenery, adrenaline, and culture all in one.

  • Turquoise glacial whitewater
  • Horseback & zip-line add-ons
  • Boutique riverside lodge lodging
  • Max 10 guests per expedition

Sun Kosi River — Nepal

Class IV

📍 Nepal, Himalayan Foothills  |  10 Days  |  From $2,400/person

One of the top ten river journeys in the world, the Sun Kosi winds through remote Himalayan foothills with virtually no road access — just pure, untouched wilderness. Each day brings new gorges, vibrant villages, and pumping rapids. End the journey with optional trekking in the Kathmandu Valley. A spiritual and physical adventure like no other.

  • 270km through remote Himalayan terrain
  • Village cultural immersion
  • Optional Kathmandu trekking
  • Max 14 guests per expedition
